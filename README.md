## About

Data engineer with 5+ years in data and BI. I currently own the analytics layer of a multi-tenant SaaS platform: the data lake, the models and reports customers rely on, and the automation around them.

I like building things end to end: designing new reports and metrics, automating manual work, and proposing better ways to deliver data. I also care about the foundation underneath, so what I ship stays accurate, fast and easy to maintain.

## What I work on

- **Building data products.** New reports, metrics and semantic models for customers and internal teams, from requirements to delivery.
- **Automation.** Serverless reporting and pipelines on AWS, defined as code and covered by tests, replacing manual work.
- **Platform improvements.** Proposing and shipping changes such as materialized views, indexing and refresh strategy on PostgreSQL/Aurora that make data faster and fresher.
- **Data quality.** Validation, reconciliation and root-cause analysis so teams can trust what they see.
- **New capabilities.** Prototyping ideas such as an LLM-based data assistant with read-only guardrails and tenant isolation.
